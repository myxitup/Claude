# Fact bank

Checked on the web in November 2026. **Re-verify big stats before each episode** (they change yearly). Every FALSE claim follows the fairness rule: wrong by a mile, the opposite, or the wrong thing entirely.

Status: ✅ ready · ⚠️ use with the caveat noted

---

## FALSE claims (believable, but way off)

### History
| Claim | Reveal | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ "The shortest war in history lasted 3 days." | Anglo-Zanzibar War, 1896: **38–45 minutes**. | [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/event/Anglo-Zanzibar-War) |
| ✅ "Medieval people drank beer all day because water was deadly." | A myth: they drank water when it was clean; beer was food and pay. | [HowStuffWorks](https://history.howstuffworks.com/medieval-people-drink-beer-water.htm), [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/no-medieval-people-didnt-drink-booze-to-avoid-dirty-w-1533442326) |
| ✅ "Cleopatra was Egyptian by blood." | Macedonian Greek family; likely the first of her dynasty to learn Egyptian. | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/10-little-known-facts-about-cleopatra) |
| ✅ "The Great Wall is held together with sticky rice, and it's still edible." | Sticky-rice lime mortar is real (Ming era); it's not food. | [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1423508/historical-connection-sticky-rice-great-wall-of-china/) |
| ✅ "The Great Pyramid had gold-covered glass windows." | Cased in polished white Tura limestone that shone. | [National Museums Scotland](https://nms.ac.uk/discover-catalogue/casing-stone-from-the-great-pyramid-of-giza) |

### Laws and society
| Claim | Reveal | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ "In Japan it's illegal to be overweight, and you can be fined." | "Metabo Law" (2008) measures waists at 40–74; penalties hit companies and local governments, not people. | [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/the-metabo-law/) |
| ✅ "Chewing gum is illegal in Singapore." | Selling/importing is banned (1992); chewing isn't; medical gum allowed. | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chewing_gum_sales_ban_in_Singapore) |
| ✅ "Pilots and co-pilots eat the same meal for good luck." | Many airlines make them eat different meals so food poisoning can't take out both. | [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/travel/why-pilots-and-co-pilots-dont-eat-the-same-meals-on-a-flight) |

### Animals
| Claim | Reveal | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ "Hippos sweat blood." | Red secretion: neither blood nor sweat; works as sunscreen and antibiotic. | [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/429363a) |
| ✅ "Dolphins never sleep, or they'd drown." | They sleep half a brain at a time, one eye open. | [Scientific American](https://scientificamerican.com/article/meet-the-animals-that-literally-sleep-with-one-eye-open) |
| ✅ "A koala was once arrested because its fingerprints matched a crime scene." | Prints really are near-identical to ours; no such case exists. | [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/koala-fingerprints-crime-scene/) |
| ✅ "Wombats poop cubes because their bottoms are square." | Cubes form in the last 8% of the gut (stiff and stretchy sections of the wall). | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/scientists-have-solved-mystery-how-wombats-poop-cubes-180976898/) |
| ⚠️ "Vets now prescribe purring sessions because purrs heal bones." | Purr frequencies overlap vibration therapy, but it's unproven and not prescribed. | [Chewy](https://be.chewy.com/?p=9427) |
| ✅ "Scientists found an immortal jellyfish alive since the dinosaurs." | *Turritopsis dohrnii* can revert to a polyp, but most are eaten or die of disease. | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turritopsis_dohrnii) |

### Places
| Claim | Reveal | Source |

| ⚠️ "Swim in Australia's pink lake and your skin turns pink for good." | Colour most likely from salt-loving algae (not fully confirmed); doesn't stain. | [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Hillier) |

### The body
| Claim | Reveal | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ "Your stomach grows a new lining every 4 hours." | Every 3–5 days. | [Univ. of Aberdeen](https://abdn.ac.uk/medical/elf/courses/view/126314/a-short-review-of-histology/1/page100) |
| ✅ "Burn off your fingerprints and they grow back in a new pattern." | Shallow burns regrow the same; only deep scarring changes them. | [Today I Found Out](https://www.todayifoundout.com/index.php/2014/07/fingerprints-form-can-regenerate/) |
| ✅ "Astronauts shrink about 5 cm in space." | They grow up to ~3% (≈5 cm). | [Space.com](https://www.space.com/19116-astronauts-taller-space-spines.html) |

### Space and science
| Claim | Reveal | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ "Clocks on the Moon run 5 minutes faster a day." | ~56–59 **microseconds**; lunar time standard ordered in 2024. | [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/space/space-exploration/nasa-to-create-a-new-time-zone-for-the-moon-by-2026) |
| ✅ "Earth has a second permanent moon that orbits every 500 years." | Temporary mini-moons only; 2024 PT5 stayed 56 days. | [Space.com](https://www.space.com/earth-mini-moon-asteroid-2024-pt5) |
| ✅ "It rains diamonds on Saturn's moon Titan." | Titan rains methane; diamond rain is a theory for Neptune/Uranus. | [Astronomy](https://www.astronomy.com/science/uranus-and-neptune-cloudy-with-a-chance-of-diamonds/) |
| ✅ "Trees scream when thirsty, and you can hear it on your phone." | Clicks at 40–80 kHz; humans hear up to ~16 kHz. | [Tel Aviv Univ.](https://english.tau.ac.il/node/3334) |
| ✅ "Scientists 3D-printed a human heart and transplanted it in 2023." | 2019: rabbit-sized, couldn't pump, no transplant. | [Tel Aviv Univ.](https://english.tau.ac.il/news/printed_heart) |
