# Round 2: Finland's €121,000 speeding ticket (TRUE)

Model round for the **"big disbelief" moment** and for a round where **Ricky gets it right**.

## Script

> **NARRATOR:** True or false? A man in Finland got a speeding ticket for over **one hundred thousand euros**.
>
> **RICKY** *(full-screen close-up, ≈2 s, no pause):* "[skeptical] A hundred grand? For speeding? Prove it."
>
> **NARRATOR:** In most countries, a speeding ticket costs the same for everyone. But in Finland, fines are counted in "day-fines"... and what a day-fine costs depends on how much you earn.
>
> **RICKY** *(YouTube pause, standing, the big disbelief moment, up to 6–8 s):* "[incredulous] Wait, wait, wait. So if man's rich, man has to pay more than someone who's broke... even though we got caught doing the same speed? [scoffs] Nah. Nah, that can't be legal."
> *(The user's own line, lightly edited: "wait so if man's rich, man would have to pay more than someone who is poor even though we got caught doing the same speed..nah...")*
>
> **NARRATOR:** So the bigger your salary... the bigger your ticket. Which raises a question: what happens when a *very* rich man gets caught?
>
> **NARRATOR:** True or false? Lock it in.
>
> **RICKY** *(close-up, over the countdown):* "[thinking] Finland don't play, still... but a hundred grand? I dunno, man."
>
> **RICKY** *(box, standing):* "[skeptical] ...Aight. I believe it."  → pick: TRUE
>
> **NARRATOR:** It's true. A Finnish businessman was fined about **one hundred and twenty-one thousand euros**, because his fine was based on his income.
>
> **RICKY** *(full-screen, excited):* "[laughs] Told you, man! ...Lowkey glad I'm broke."
>
> Score: **RICKY 1 : 0 FACTS**

## Why it works
- Ricky voices exactly what the viewer is thinking, and his rant **explains the system** while he complains.
- He's **right** this time, so viewers can't just bet against him.
- The reveal pays off the rant ("glad I'm broke").

## Facts (checked)
- Finland uses **day-fines**: the number of day-fines depends on how serious the offence is; the amount per day-fine depends on the offender's daily disposable income. In place since the 1920s.
- A Finnish businessman was fined about **€121,000** for speeding (14 day-fines).
- Sources: [Yahoo News](https://news.yahoo.com/finnish-businessman-handed-121-000-125636215.html), [World Economic Forum](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2018/06/in-finland-speeding-tickets-are-linked-to-your-income/).
- Left out on purpose: the exact speed and year (sources disagree).
